# RDC Business Hub

Concept website for **RDC Business Hub — Your Business. Your Beirut Office.**

## V1
Static, fast, responsive launch website designed for temporary GitHub Pages hosting while the final domain, legal service wording, pricing and photography are finalized.

### Product direction
- Virtual Office
- Business Presence
- Executive / Serviced Offices
- Meeting & Board Rooms
- Flexible workspace
- RDC business services

The site intentionally avoids promising statutory registered-office eligibility until Lebanese legal/accounting requirements are confirmed.
